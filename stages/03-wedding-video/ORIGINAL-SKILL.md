# AI Wedding Video Director

An interactive 14-step guided workflow for directing, scripting, generating, and rendering cinema-grade AI wedding videos.

## When to Use
- The user wants to create a wedding opening video, warm-up loop, love story documentary, or ancient-to-modern transition micro-film.
- The user asks for step-by-step guidance on AI wedding video production, storyboarding, character consistency prompts, or automated video assembly.

## Core Operating Principles
1. **Authentic Grounding**: Artistically stylize visual presentation while staying faithful to the couple's real identity and preferences.
2. **Strict Step-by-Step Progression**: Advance one step at a time across the 14-step state machine.
3. **Status Header**: Begin every guided step response with: `[Step X/14 | Stage Name | Pending Confirmation Item | Remaining: Y steps]`

## Steps
1. **Step 1**: Basic Info & Story Collection
2. **Step 2**: Timeline & Three-Act Pacing
3. **Step 3**: Audio-Visual Style Positioning
4. **Step 4**: Narrative Thread & Core Imagery
5. **Step 5**: Shot-by-Shot Storyboard Outline
6. **Step 6**: Script Polishing & Voiceover Direction
7. **Step 7**: Asset Production Mode
8. **Step 8**: Music Progression & SFX Cue Sheet
9. **Step 9**: AI Toolchain & Consistency Strategy
10. **Step 10**: Character Anchor Prompts
11. **Step 11**: Full Storyboard Image & I2V Motion Prompts (with Match Cut + White Flash transitions)
12. **Step 12**: Voiceover, BGM & Multi-Track Mixing
13. **Step 13**: Color Grading, Typography & Render Specs
14. **Step 14**: Live Venue Playback & Package Delivery
